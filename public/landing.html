Guests scan the QR code on their table and order in their own language. Your waiters approve, your kitchen cooks from a clear screen, and you see every order in one daily report.

No card needed. No app for guests. Works on the phones and tablets you already have.

🌍 Six languages

English, Arabic, Dari, Pashto, Urdu and Hindi, right-to-left included. You choose which ones your guests see.

👨‍🍳 Kitchen display

New, cooking, ready. Timers, late-ticket alerts and a sound for each new order. Nothing gets lost on paper.

🛎 Waiter calls

Guests call a waiter, ask for the bill or water. Requests waiting over 2 minutes turn red.

🧾 Tax ready

Your currency and tax, included in prices or added on top. UAE setup with 5% VAT in one click.

📊 Daily report

Orders, value, cancellations with reasons, kitchen and waiter speed, best-selling dishes.

🔒 Guest privacy

Guests never create an account. No names, phone numbers or cards are collected.

Up and running in an afternoon

  1. Sign up and choose your restaurant's web address.
  2. Add your menu, prices and translations. Start from our sample menu if you like.
  3. Print the QR codes for your tables and add a PIN for each waiter and cook.
  4. Take orders. Payments stay at your counter, as today.
