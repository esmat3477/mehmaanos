MehmaanOS staff guide

Before service (manager, 5 min)

  1. Open /admin. Mark sold-out dishes (untick "available").
  2. Check the kitchen tablet shows ● live in green.
  3. Check every table has its QR card. A missing or damaged card → Admin → Tables & QR → print.
  4. Keep 20 paper order slips (last page) at the counter.

پیش از کار: غذاهای تمام‌شده را خاموش کنید، «live» سبز را روی تبلت آشپزخانه ببینید، کارت QR هر میز را بررسی کنید.

Waiter (/staff)

  1. New order appears under "Needs approval". Check the table is really occupied and the order makes sense → Accept → kitchen. If not → Reject and write a short reason.
  2. Guest request (waiter, bill, water): tap I'm on it, go to the table, then Done. Red = the guest has waited over 2 minutes.
  3. When food is Ready, take it to the table → Mark served.
  4. When the guest has paid at the counter → Close (paid).
  5. Guest wants to change an order already in the kitchen: tell the kitchen in person, Cancel with the reason, ask the guest to send a new order (or take it on paper).

پیشخدمت: سفارش نو را تأیید یا رد کنید، به درخواست مهمان «I'm on it» بزنید، غذای آماده را ببرید و «served» بزنید، بعد از پرداخت «Close» بزنید.

Kitchen (/kitchen)

  1. New tickets appear in New with a sound. Tap Start cooking when you begin.
  2. Tap Mark ready when the plate is ready for the waiter.
  3. Read notes (📝) and spice level on every line. A red border means the ticket is over 20 minutes old.
  4. Never cook from a guest's phone screen. Only cook what is on this display or on a paper slip.

آشپزخانه: «Start cooking» را بزنید، وقتی آماده شد «Mark ready». یادداشت و میزان تندی را بخوانید.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
Kitchen screen says reconnecting for more than 1 minuteManager: Admin → Settings → Pause QR ordering. Switch to paper slips. Tell waiters.
No internet at allPaper slips for everyone. Guests can still read the menu if it already loaded; tell them to order with the waiter.
Guest says "my order didn't go through"Check /staff for their table before re-ordering. The system never sends the same order twice, but the guest may have sent two different orders.
Strange orders from a table nobody sits atReject them. Admin → Tables & QR → New QR for that table and put the new card out.
Wrong price on the menuAdmin → Menu → Edit. Orders already placed keep the old price; correct at the till.
Guest asks about allergiesAlways ask the kitchen. The menu list helps but the cook confirms.

اگر اینترنت قطع شد: در Admin سفارش QR را متوقف کنید و از برگه‌های کاغذی استفاده کنید.

End of day (manager)

  1. /staff: every order should be Closed, Rejected or Canceled. Close what is left.
  2. Admin → Daily report: compare "Closed (paid)" with the till. Print or save it.
  3. Collect used paper slips and add them to the day's paperwork.
